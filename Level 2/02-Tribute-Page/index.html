01Her story

A dream that
served a nation.

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam rose from a modest childhood to become one of India's most beloved scientists and leaders.

An aerospace scientist, Kalam contributed to India's civilian space programme and the development of its missile capabilities. He played a central role in the successful launch of the SLV-III, which placed the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980.

In 2002, he became the 11th President of India. Known as the “People's President,” he remained a teacher at heart, encouraging young people to pursue knowledge and work toward a stronger, more inclusive India.

    Abdul Kalam is born in Rameswaram, in what is now Tamil Nadu.

  2. 1980

    India reaches orbit

    The SLV-III project, led by Kalam, successfully places the Rohini satellite in orbit.

  3. 1997

    Bharat Ratna

    He receives India's highest civilian award for his contributions to science and public service.

  4. 2002

    The People's President

    Kalam is sworn in as the 11th President of India, serving until 2007.
